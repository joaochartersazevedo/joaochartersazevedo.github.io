How it's built

Client

Flutter and Dart with Riverpod. One codebase for iOS and Android, covered by more than 130 test files.

Backend

Supabase: Postgres with row-level security, over 360 versioned migrations, and Deno edge functions in TypeScript.

Game economy

Rewards, purchases and boss fights resolve on the server, so the app can't be tricked into granting itself coins.

Payments

Subscriptions through RevenueCat, kept in sync with the backend by webhooks.

Releases

Fastlane and GitHub Actions ship to both stores. I handle App Review and Play policy cycles myself.

Master's thesis

Dialogue for game characters, chosen from an emotion wheel instead of a menu.

Enhancing Visual Novel Storytelling through Personality-Based Dialogue Systems Powered by Large Language Models. Instituto Superior Técnico, supervised by Prof. Carlos Martinho. Graded 16 out of 20.

Players pick one of 16 emotions on a wheel. A language model writes the line in the character's voice from their personality, relationships and story context, and the designer reviews every line before it ships. I built it as a Flutter desktop app with an editor and a playable engine.

Tested with 8 participants. Editor usability scored 77.5 on the System Usability Scale (the usual pass mark is 68), and the play experience scored +1.49 on the User Experience Questionnaire, positive on all six scales.
